\section{Motivation}

Companion~G of the FDS--G1 series proposes a source--response--optics decomposition
of dark-matter phenomenology in finite-screen gravity~\cite{FDSG1}.  The central
analytic claim is that dark-sector observables must be separated into three
independent channels:
\begin{equation}
\mathcal D_{\rm obs}
=
T_{\mu\nu}^{D}\;\oplus\; R_{\mu\nu}^{D}\;\oplus\; \delta W_{\rm opt}^{D},
\end{equation}
where $T_{\mu\nu}^{D}$ is a dark carrier-source, $R_{\mu\nu}^{D}$ is a
density-like screen-response residual, and $\delta W_{\rm opt}^{D}$ is a
Weyl/optical residual.

If this decomposition is not enforced, a single phenomenological preference (e.g., a
nonzero $\Sigma_0$ in a compressed $\mu\Sigma$ fit) can be misinterpreted as a
confirmed dark-matter sector.  Companion~G therefore demands that source, response,
and optics be separately identifiable before any G1 dark-matter claim is admitted.

This technical note reports the first two public-chain diagnostics from the
\texttt{g1dm\_data\_notes} toolkit (v0.2.1).  Together they test whether
current public cosmological data require (i)~a source-like dark carrier floor
and (ii)~a modified-growth (Ricci-leakage) response.

\section{Note~1: Planck Carrier Floor}

The carrier-floor test uses Planck~2018 TT,TE,EE+lowE chains~\cite{Planck2018} to
ask whether $\Omega_c h^2=0$ is excluded by CMB data.  If
$\Omega_c h^2=0$ were allowed, a pure Weyl/optical residual could in principle
replace the full CDM sector.  If it is excluded, a source-like carrier floor is
required.

$\Omega_c h^2=0$ lies far outside the Planck posterior support.  The posterior
is concentrated near $\Omega_c h^2\simeq0.12$ with an effective exclusion

$\Omega_c h^2=0.1200\pm0.0012$; the exact numerical value of the $z$-score is
insensitive to the precise chain realization).

\vspace{0.3em}
\noindent\textbf{Diagnosis.}\;
Planck~2018 CMB data require a nonzero CDM-like carrier floor.
Pure Weyl/optical residuals cannot replace the full CDM sector.
In Companion~G notation, $T_{\mu\nu}^{D}\neq0$.

\section{Note~3: DESI DR1 Lensing-Growth Split}

The lensing-growth split test uses DESI DR1 \texttt{base\_mu\_sigma} public
chains from the DESI DR1 full-shape cosmology repository~\cite{DESIDR1FS}.
In the DESI/ISiTGR $\mu\Sigma$ convention used by these chains,
$\Sigma(a)=1+\sigma_0^{\rm DESI}\,f(a)$ where $\sigma_0^{\rm DESI}>0$ corresponds to
enhanced lensing relative to GR.  This sign convention is opposite to the current
M$_{3/4}$ sign prediction for $s<3$.

Two dataset combinations are available in the public \texttt{cobaya} chains:
(i)~FS/BAO+Planck only and (ii)~FS/BAO+Planck+DESY3joint.
A third combination (FS/BAO+Planck+DESY3joint+DESY5SN) is not yet available as a
\texttt{base\_mu\_sigma} \texttt{cobaya} chain in the DESI DR1 VAC.

\subsection{Compressed Gaussian comparison}

For each chain, the weighted mean and covariance of $( \mu_0, \sigma_0^{\rm DESI} )$
are extracted from the posterior samples.  Four models are compared in the
compressed 2D Gaussian space:
\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{GR}  ---  $(\mu_0,\sigma_0^{\rm DESI}) = (0,0)$;
\item \textbf{Growth-only}  ---  $(\mu_0,\sigma_0^{\rm DESI}) = (\mbox{free},0)$;
\item \textbf{Weyl-only}  ---  $(\mu_0,\sigma_0^{\rm DESI}) = (0,\mbox{free})$;
\item \textbf{Growth+Weyl}  ---  $(\mu_0,\sigma_0^{\rm DESI}) = (\mbox{free},\mbox{free})$.
\end{itemize}

Model comparison uses compressed Gaussian BIC (with an adopted
effective-penalty convention) and $z$-scores for the growth and Weyl residuals.

\vspace{0.3em}
\noindent\textbf{Diagnosis.}\;
The strong Weyl residual in FS/BAO+Planck ($3.86\sigma$) is consistent with the
known Planck PR3 lensing anomaly~\cite{Planck2018} and with the official DESI MG
paper's finding of GR-consistent results when DESY3+DESY5SN are added.  After DES~Y3
$3\times2$pt data are included, $\sigma_0^{\rm DESI}$ returns to consistency with zero
($0.96\sigma$).  Growth leakage ($\mu_0\neq0$) is not required in either combination.

\section{Implications for M\textsubscript{3/4} and Companion~G}

These results support the G1DE/M$_{3/4}$ channel hierarchy but do not confirm the full
M$_{3/4}$ Weyl sign/amplitude lock:

\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{Suppressed growth leakage confirmed.}\;
  $\mu_0$ is consistent with zero in both chain combinations, supporting
  the M$_{3/4}$ claim that the leading residual is not Ricci/growth-dominated.

\item \textbf{Weyl residual not robust across dataset combinations.}\;
  The Planck-linked Weyl preference in FS/BAO+Planck disappears when DES~Y3
  $3\times2$pt data are added.  This demonstrates that a single Weyl preference
  cannot be interpreted as a confirmed M$_{3/4}$ Weyl-branch signal without
  dataset-combination robustness checks.

\item \textbf{Source--response--optics identifiability justified.}\;
  The fact that a strong Weyl signal in one combination collapses under the addition
  of independent lensing data vindicates Companion~G's requirement that
  source, response, and optics be separately identifiable before any G1
  dark-matter claim is admitted.
\end{itemize}
