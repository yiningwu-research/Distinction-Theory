# Phase 3: KiDS BandPower EE+nE compressed-space bridge — COMPLETE / PASS
---
## Executive Summary
Phase 3 completes the KiDS BandPower (EE+nE) compressed-space bridge. The validated bridge combines the 120-row PeeE shear-shear channel and the 80-row PneE density-shear channel into the full 200-row KiDS BandPower product, with verified row ordering, covariance usage, projection conventions, sign alignment, and finite G1 model-smoke predictions. These results are infrastructure and diagnostic validation only; they are not an optimized likelihood, not model evidence, and not a full 3×2pt result because the (nn) clustering channel remains pending.
